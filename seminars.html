EPHOD Church Strengthening Seminars

Thirteen seminars that show you how to be a true disciple and disciple maker of Jesus Christ.

Herb and Karen Johnson spent more than ten years developing EPHOD, and the result is an extensive, practical course covering almost all of the foundational principles of the Christian life. The seminars have been presented in churches across Cambodia, the Philippines and Papua New Guinea, and on DVD and online.

A new online version of the full course is being prepared for this site, with every session on video. If you would like to know when it opens, or you want to bring the seminars to your church now, get in touch.
