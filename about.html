About EPHOD

EPHOD is an acronym: Every Person, Household, Oikos — Discipled. Oikos is the Greek word for a household and the circle of people around it. The vision is that every person, every family and every circle of friends is discipled in the Word of God.

EPHOD is a discipleship program developed by Rev. Herb and Rev. Karen Johnson during thirty years of missionary ministry with the Assemblies of God in the Philippines and Papua New Guinea. As missionaries they saw how much the churches needed practical discipleship training, and over more than ten years they shaped what they learned into a program that helps pastors train believers "for works of service" (Ephesians 4:11–12).

The heart of it is simple. A disciple continues in God's Word (John 8:31) and goes to make disciples (Matthew 28:19). The EPHOD 10 Point Plan turns that into daily habits: reading the Bible every morning and evening, memorizing a verse each week, keeping a journal, meeting in small groups, prayer and fasting, giving, and sharing Jesus with the people God has placed around you.

The full program is presented as the EPHOD Church Strengthening Seminars, thirteen seminars that have been taught to hundreds of people in Cambodia, the Philippines, Papua New Guinea and beyond — including villages deep in the PNG highlands with no electricity or running water.

The daily reading plan

Part of the program is a one-year Bible reading plan: an Old Testament passage each morning and a New Testament passage each evening. Follow it from January to December and you have read the whole Bible, with the prophets in the order they lived and the New Testament arranged so that the story keeps coming back to the life, death and resurrection of Jesus. The plan is the Full Life Study Bible Reading Plan, used by permission of Life Publishers International.

This website, and the EPHOD PH and EPHOD PNG apps, exist so that anyone, anywhere, can follow that plan — with a weekly memory verse, a daily Gold Nugget verse, and a devotion each week.

The apps

EPHOD PH and EPHOD PNG were built by Kevin Johnson, Herb and Karen's son and the missions coordinator at Victorville First Assembly of God, so that the reading plan could live on a phone. He launched EPHOD PNG in Papua New Guinea in person. Many believers there walked to a top-up store and bought a day's worth of data just to download the app; some had come from villages where the signal carries only calls and texts. That is why the apps work entirely offline once the Bible is on the phone, and why they are free, with no ads and nothing to buy.
