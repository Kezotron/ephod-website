The EPHOD apps

The daily readings, the weekly memory verse, the Gold Nugget, a journal and a progress tracker — on your phone, free, with no ads and nothing to buy. Download the Bible once and every day's reading works without a connection.

EPHOD PH

For the Philippines and the world. The app speaks English, Tagalog and Chinese, and offers the King James Version, World English Bible, American Standard Version, Bible in Basic English, Ang Dating Biblia and Chinese translations.

EPHOD PNG

For Papua New Guinea, and Papua New Guineans in Australia and the Solomon Islands. The app speaks English and Tok Pisin, and offers Buk Baibel long Tok Pisin, the public-domain English versions, and more than 200 Papua New Guinea language Bibles, each downloaded only when you choose it.

Get it onGoogle Play Download on theApp Store

What the apps do

  • The morning and evening reading for every day of the year, in the version you choose
  • The weekly memory verse and the daily Gold Nugget
  • A journal for your thoughts on each day's reading
  • A progress tracker that keeps your streak
  • Reminders for the morning reading, the evening reading and the Gold Nugget
  • Everything offline once a Bible version is downloaded

Scripture is read as each day's passages; the apps are not a searchable Bible. They collect no personal information and have no accounts. Privacy policy.

Finding them in the store

Several unrelated apps are also called "EPHOD", so search for EPHOD PH or EPHOD PNG, or use the buttons on this page, which go straight to the listings.
