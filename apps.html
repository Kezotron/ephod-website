The EPHOD PNG app

The Daily Readings, the weekly Memory Verse, the Gold Nugget, a Journal and a Progress Tracker — on your phone, free, with no ads and nothing to buy. Download the Bible once and every day's reading works without a connection.

Discipleship doesn't stop when you leave the house. With the app, EPHOD goes wherever you go — the morning reading over breakfast, the memory verse on the bus, the evening reading before bed — all day long, wherever you are.

What the app does

  • The morning and evening reading for every day of the year, in the version you choose
  • The weekly Memory Verse and the daily Gold Nugget
  • A Journal for your thoughts on each day's reading
  • A Progress Tracker that keeps your streak
  • Reminders for the morning reading, the evening reading and the Gold Nugget
  • Everything offline once a Bible version is downloaded

Scripture is read as each day's passages; the app is not a searchable Bible. It collects no personal information and has no accounts. Privacy policy.

EPHOD PNG

For Papua New Guinea, and for Papua New Guineans in Australia and the Solomon Islands. The app speaks English and Tok Pisin, and offers Buk Baibel long Tok Pisin, the public-domain English versions, and more than 200 Papua New Guinea language Bibles — each downloaded only when you choose it, so you can read the daily passage in your own tokples. Where a passage has not yet been translated into your language, the app shows it in Tok Pisin.
